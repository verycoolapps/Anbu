# Public Social Media Research

> ANBU OSINT Division 002 — Collection Tradecraft

## Purpose

Defines ethical and effective OSINT collection from public social media: public posts, profiles of public figures and institutions, and trending public conversations.

## Scope

Public posts and profiles on major platforms (X, LinkedIn, Facebook public pages, Instagram public, TikTok public, Reddit, Telegram public channels, YouTube), focusing on public-interest subjects.

## Intelligence objectives

- Monitor public statements by officials, organizations, and public figures
- Track public discourse on events and narratives
- Identify network and coordination patterns in public posts
- Preserve ephemeral public content before deletion

## Core concepts

- Public versus private account distinction
- Handle and identity verification
- Ephemeral content and deletion patterns
- Platform API versus manual public viewing
- Graph and network analysis of public interactions

## Analytical methodology

Collect only content visible without login where possible; where login is required, use the account holder's own lawful access and respect platform terms. Verify account authenticity before attribution. Preserve posts with timestamps and URLs; archive where permitted. Analyze at aggregate level; avoid building personal profiles of private individuals.

## Step-by-step workflow

1. Identify public accounts of interest (officials, institutions, public figures)
2. Verify account authenticity and affiliation
3. Collect public posts within platform terms
4. Archive critical items before deletion
5. Analyze content, timing, and network patterns
6. Log with platform, handle, timestamp, and collection method

## Data requirements

Target accounts and hashtags; period; platforms; lawful access method; ethical scope definition.

## Approved public sources

- Public posts on X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram
- Public Telegram channels and groups
- Reddit public subreddits
- YouTube public videos and comments
- Platform trend and search features

## Source verification standards

Every source is evaluated for authority, proximity to the event or subject, currency, and internal consistency before its content is used analytically. Primary sources are preferred over secondary accounts. Where sources conflict, the conflict is preserved and reported rather than silently resolved. Source reliability is rated on the ANBU reliability scale and recorded in the collection log.

## Analytical framework

Social media content is evidence of what was publicly posted, by whom, when. Authenticity of the account is established before attribution. Deletion patterns are noted. Analysis focuses on public-interest actors and discourse, not private individuals.

## Evidence requirements

Every analytical claim must be traceable to a logged collection item. Evidence requirements: (1) verbatim quotation or precise paraphrase with page/section reference; (2) source attribution including URL or archive identifier; (3) collection timestamp; (4) access method; (5) confidence grade per the ANBU scale. Claims without logged evidence are marked INSUFFICIENT and excluded from analytical products.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH: multiple independent primary sources, direct documentation, no material contradictions. MODERATE: credible sources with minor gaps or single-source primary documentation. LOW: indirect, derivative, or partially corroborated reporting. INSUFFICIENT: evidence does not support a judgment; collection gap or contested sources prevent assessment.

## Common analytical mistakes

- Attributing posts to wrong or fake accounts
- Collecting private individuals' data
- Ignoring platform terms and rate limits
- Treating viral claims as verified without corroboration

## Ethical considerations

Strict boundary: public-interest subjects only. No doxxing, no stalking, no collection of private individuals' personal data, no fake engagement, no platform manipulation. Respect platform terms of service; where terms restrict collection, manual public viewing is preferred over automated extraction.

## Security boundaries

No credential stuffing, no fake accounts for infiltration, no bypass of privacy settings, no scraping of non-public content, no harassment tooling.

## Expected output

Social media collection log with posts, accounts, timestamps, authenticity assessments, and analysis notes.

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Account authenticity verified
- [ ] Public visibility confirmed
- [ ] Terms of service respected
- [ ] No private individual data collected
