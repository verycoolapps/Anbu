# Public Document Analysis

> ANBU OSINT Division 002 — Collection Tradecraft

## Purpose

Provides methodology for analyzing public documents: official reports, white papers, NGO reports, court documents, and institutional publications.

## Scope

Analysis of document corpora for content, framing, consistency, and evidentiary value.

## Intelligence objectives

- Extract findings and claims from document collections
- Assess document authority and evidentiary weight
- Detect inconsistencies across documents
- Build document timelines for event reconstruction

## Core concepts

- Document provenance and chain of custody
- Authored versus leaked documents
- Classification markings and declassification status
- Document genre conventions (report, memo, brief, transcript)

## Analytical methodology

Catalog documents by issuer, date, classification, and genre. Extract text via lawful means (PDF extraction, OCR). Analyze structure, language, and claims. Cross-reference claims across documents and against other sources. Assess author authority and institutional context. Flag inconsistencies and contradictions.

## Step-by-step workflow

1. Inventory document collection
2. Classify by issuer, date, genre, classification
3. Extract and normalize text
4. Analyze content, structure, and claims
5. Cross-reference across documents and sources
6. Produce analysis with confidence grading

## Data requirements

Document collection; issuer context; date range; analytical question; related sources for cross-reference.

## Approved public sources

- Government reports and white papers
- Court documents and legal filings
- NGO and civil society reports
- Institutional publications and audits
- Parliamentary committee reports
- Leaked documents that are publicly available (treated with provenance caution)

## Source verification standards

Every source is evaluated for authority, proximity to the event or subject, currency, and internal consistency before its content is used analytically. Primary sources are preferred over secondary accounts. Where sources conflict, the conflict is preserved and reported rather than silently resolved. Source reliability is rated on the ANBU reliability scale and recorded in the collection log.

## Analytical framework

Documents are graded by issuer authority, classification status, corroboration, and internal consistency. Leaked documents are labeled as such with provenance assessment. Claims are extracted verbatim where possible; paraphrases are flagged.

## Evidence requirements

Every analytical claim must be traceable to a logged collection item. Evidence requirements: (1) verbatim quotation or precise paraphrase with page/section reference; (2) source attribution including URL or archive identifier; (3) collection timestamp; (4) access method; (5) confidence grade per the ANBU scale. Claims without logged evidence are marked INSUFFICIENT and excluded from analytical products.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH: multiple independent primary sources, direct documentation, no material contradictions. MODERATE: credible sources with minor gaps or single-source primary documentation. LOW: indirect, derivative, or partially corroborated reporting. INSUFFICIENT: evidence does not support a judgment; collection gap or contested sources prevent assessment.

## Common analytical mistakes

- Treating all documents as equally authoritative
- Ignoring classification and declassification context
- Failing to check document authenticity
- Missing genre conventions that affect interpretation

## Ethical considerations

Analyze documents lawfully obtained. Leaked documents are analyzed with explicit provenance caveats; never solicit or traffic in stolen documents.

## Security boundaries

No solicitation of classified or stolen documents. No publication of sensitive personal data found in documents.

## Expected output

Document analysis report with claim inventory, consistency assessment, provenance notes, and confidence-graded findings.

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Document provenance established
- [ ] Issuer authority assessed
- [ ] Claims extracted verbatim
- [ ] Inconsistencies flagged
