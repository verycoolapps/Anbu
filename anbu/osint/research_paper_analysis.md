# Research Paper Analysis

> ANBU OSINT Division 002 — Collection Tradecraft

## Purpose

Provides methodology for deep analysis of research papers: methodology assessment, findings extraction, and credibility evaluation.

## Scope

Analysis of individual papers and paper corpora for intelligence-relevant findings, technical capabilities, and expert assessment.

## Intelligence objectives

- Evaluate methodology rigor and statistical validity
- Extract findings and their stated limitations
- Assess author and institutional credibility
- Identify contradictions between papers and other sources

## Core concepts

- Study design hierarchy: RCT, cohort, case-control, observational
- Effect size versus statistical significance
- Conflict of interest and funding disclosure
- Replication status and citation context

## Analytical methodology

Read full text, not abstract alone. Extract: research question, design, sample, method, results, limitations, funding, conflicts. Assess design against the claim made. Check for retractions, corrections, and failed replications via Retraction Watch and citation context. Compare findings across papers on the same topic.

## Step-by-step workflow

1. Obtain full text lawfully
2. Extract methodology and findings into structured notes
3. Assess design rigor and stated limitations
4. Check retraction/correction status
5. Compare with related papers and other source classes
6. Write analysis with confidence grading

## Data requirements

Full text access; bibliographic metadata; knowledge of the analytical question the paper informs.

## Approved public sources

- Publisher full text and open access versions
- Retraction Watch database
- Citation indexes for context
- Author institutional pages
- Supplementary materials and datasets

## Source verification standards

Every source is evaluated for authority, proximity to the event or subject, currency, and internal consistency before its content is used analytically. Primary sources are preferred over secondary accounts. Where sources conflict, the conflict is preserved and reported rather than silently resolved. Source reliability is rated on the ANBU reliability scale and recorded in the collection log.

## Analytical framework

Papers are scored on design appropriateness, sample adequacy, analytical transparency, and disclosure quality. Findings are reported with the paper's own stated limitations. Contradictions between papers are preserved as contested findings, not resolved by preference.

## Evidence requirements

Every analytical claim must be traceable to a logged collection item. Evidence requirements: (1) verbatim quotation or precise paraphrase with page/section reference; (2) source attribution including URL or archive identifier; (3) collection timestamp; (4) access method; (5) confidence grade per the ANBU scale. Claims without logged evidence are marked INSUFFICIENT and excluded from analytical products.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH: multiple independent primary sources, direct documentation, no material contradictions. MODERATE: credible sources with minor gaps or single-source primary documentation. LOW: indirect, derivative, or partially corroborated reporting. INSUFFICIENT: evidence does not support a judgment; collection gap or contested sources prevent assessment.

## Common analytical mistakes

- Accepting conclusions without examining methodology
- Ignoring stated limitations
- Treating one paper as settled science
- Failing to check retraction status

## Ethical considerations

Analysis respects copyright: quote minimally, analyze substantively. No misrepresentation of research findings.

## Security boundaries

Lawful access only; no circumvention of paywalls.

## Expected output

Structured paper analysis: design assessment, findings, limitations, credibility rating, and confidence-graded conclusions.

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Full text reviewed
- [ ] Limitations extracted
- [ ] Retraction status checked
- [ ] Confidence graded
