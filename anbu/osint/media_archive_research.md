# Media Archive Research

> ANBU OSINT Division 002 — Collection Tradecraft

## Purpose

Covers research in broadcast and media archives: television and radio archives, news footage libraries, and historical media collections.

## Scope

Publicly accessible broadcast archives, news footage repositories, and historical media collections, including national archives with media holdings.

## Intelligence objectives

- Retrieve historical broadcast coverage of events
- Access news footage and transcripts from archives
- Document how events were reported at the time
- Build temporal media context for event reconstruction

## Core concepts

- Archive versus live broadcast
- Footage licensing and access restrictions
- Transcript versus visual record
- Archive cataloging practices and gaps

## Analytical methodology

Identify archives holding relevant material (national broadcasters' archives, footage libraries, YouTube official channels, Internet Archive television). Search catalog APIs and finding aids. Record catalog identifiers, dates, and access terms. Where footage is restricted, document existence and access pathway.

## Step-by-step workflow

1. Identify relevant archives and catalogs
2. Search by date, topic, program, and person
3. Retrieve transcripts, metadata, or accessible footage
4. Record catalog IDs and access conditions
5. Log findings and note access limitations
6. Cross-reference with news_media_monitoring.md for live coverage

## Data requirements

Event dates and descriptions; programs/persons of interest; archive jurisdictions; acceptable access methods.

## Approved public sources

- National broadcaster archives (BBC, CBC, etc.)
- Internet Archive television and radio collections
- News footage libraries
- Official broadcaster YouTube and streaming channels
- National archives with AV holdings

## Source verification standards

Every source is evaluated for authority, proximity to the event or subject, currency, and internal consistency before its content is used analytically. Primary sources are preferred over secondary accounts. Where sources conflict, the conflict is preserved and reported rather than silently resolved. Source reliability is rated on the ANBU reliability scale and recorded in the collection log.

## Analytical framework

Archived broadcasts are primary records of what was aired. Transcripts are checked against catalog metadata. Archive gaps are documented: absence of footage is itself a finding about coverage priorities.

## Evidence requirements

Every analytical claim must be traceable to a logged collection item. Evidence requirements: (1) verbatim quotation or precise paraphrase with page/section reference; (2) source attribution including URL or archive identifier; (3) collection timestamp; (4) access method; (5) confidence grade per the ANBU scale. Claims without logged evidence are marked INSUFFICIENT and excluded from analytical products.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH: multiple independent primary sources, direct documentation, no material contradictions. MODERATE: credible sources with minor gaps or single-source primary documentation. LOW: indirect, derivative, or partially corroborated reporting. INSUFFICIENT: evidence does not support a judgment; collection gap or contested sources prevent assessment.

## Common analytical mistakes

- Assuming archive coverage equals comprehensive coverage
- Ignoring cataloging errors and date misattributions
- Failing to note licensing restrictions on reuse
- Missing non-English broadcast archives

## Ethical considerations

Use archives for research and citation; respect footage licensing for redistribution. Do not circumvent archive access controls.

## Security boundaries

No circumvention of archive paywalls or authentication. No redistribution of licensed footage beyond fair dealing.

## Expected output

Archive research log with catalog citations, accessible content inventory, and documented access restrictions.

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Catalog IDs recorded
- [ ] Access terms documented
- [ ] Transcript accuracy verified
- [ ] Archive gaps noted
