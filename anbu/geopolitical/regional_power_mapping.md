# Regional Power Mapping
**Division:** Geopolitical | **Type:** Analytical Reference | **Version:** 1.0
## 1. Purpose

- Produce a graded map of power and influence within a defined region — states, institutions, and non-state actors — to anchor regional security and geopolitical analysis.
## 2. Scope

- A single region (e.g., Indo-Pacific, Europe, Middle East, Africa, Latin America) or sub-region. Global power structure is covered separately.
## 3. Intelligence objectives

- Rank regional actors by material power (military, economic, demographic).
- Map institutional and normative influence beyond material power.
- Identify external powers' footholds and penetration.
- Locate alignment structures and contested spaces within the region.
## 4. Core concepts

- Regional power hierarchy
- Penetration by external powers
- Institutional influence
- Alignment vs hedging strategies
## 5. Analytical methodology

- Regional-hierarchy framework: enumerate actors, score them on military/economic/demographic/institutional dimensions, weight by regional salience, map external influence, and grade the resulting hierarchy.
## 6. Step-by-step workflow

- Define the region's geographic and functional boundary.
- Enumerate actors: states, regional organizations, dominant non-state actors.
- Collect power indicators per actor: military spending, GDP, population, institutional roles.
- Score and rank actors; note composite vs single-dimension power.
- Map external penetration: bases, alliances, trade dependence, diplomatic presence.
- Produce the regional power map with confidence labels.
## 7. Data requirements

- SIPRI military expenditure
- World Bank and IMF economic data
- Regional organization membership and budgets
- Official defense and foreign ministry publications
- Think-tank regional studies
- UN demographic and development data
## 8. Approved public sources

- SIPRI
- World Bank
- IMF
- UN
- Regional organizations (AU, ASEAN, EU, OAS)
- CSIS
- IISS
- Chatham House
## 9. Source verification standards

- Material power is measurable; influence is interpretive — label influence assessments as ASSESSMENT.
Population and geography matter as force multipliers.
## 10. Analytical framework

- Regional-hierarchy framework: enumerate actors, score them on military/economic/demographic/institutional dimensions, weight by regional salience, map external influence, and grade the resulting hierarchy.
## 11. Evidence requirements

- Use the ANBU seven-label evidence vocabulary (FACT, VERIFIED, UNVERIFIED, ASSESSMENT, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS,
SPECULATION). Every claim must carry an evidence label. Load-bearing claims require corroboration. Inferences
must be logically derivable from cited evidence. Speculation must be explicitly labeled and confined to
clearly marked sections.
## 12. Confidence assessment

- Assess confidence on three dimensions: source confidence (trustworthiness of origin), information confidence
(reliability of the specific item), and analytical confidence (soundness of the inference). Report all three.
Use HIGH (multiple corroborated sources, strong analytical basis), MODERATE (single reliable source or limited
corroboration), LOW (weak sourcing or significant analytical uncertainty), INSUFFICIENT (inadequate evidence
to support assessment).
## 13. Common analytical mistakes

- Reducing regional power to GDP or military spending alone.
- Ignoring middle powers that punch above their weight institutionally.
- Treating external influence as uniform rather than issue-specific.
- Missing informal influence (diaspora, business networks, cultural).
## 14. Ethical considerations

- Analysis only; no targeting support for regional actors.
## 15. Security boundaries

- Public sources only. No collection on individuals; no facilitation of covert regional operations.
## 16. Expected output

- A regional power map: ranked actors with scores, influence assessments, external-penetration map, confidence
labels.
## 17. Quality checklist

- Region boundary defined
- Actors enumerated
- Power dimensions scored
- Influence vs material power distinguished
- External penetration mapped
- Confidence graded
