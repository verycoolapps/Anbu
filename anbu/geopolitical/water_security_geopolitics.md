# Water Security Geopolitics Analysis
**Division:** Geopolitical | **Type:** Analytical Reference | **Version:** 1.0
## 1. Purpose

- Analyze the geopolitics of water: transboundary rivers, groundwater, water scarcity, infrastructure disputes, and water as an instrument of statecraft.
## 2. Scope

- Transboundary water resources: rivers, aquifers, and their management — scarcity, infrastructure, and conflict/cooperation dynamics. Climate detail is covered under environmental security.
## 3. Intelligence objectives

- Map transboundary water resources and their allocation regimes.
- Assess water scarcity and its drivers (climate, population, agriculture).
- Analyze infrastructure disputes: dams, diversions, and their downstream effects.
- Evaluate water conflict vs cooperation history and future risk.
## 4. Core concepts

- Transboundary river basins
- Water as strategic resource
- Hydro-hegemony
- Upstream-downstream asymmetry
## 5. Analytical methodology

- Water-security framework: map basins and treaties, assess scarcity and drivers, analyze infrastructure disputes, and evaluate conflict/cooperation dynamics under climate and development stress.
## 6. Step-by-step workflow

- Define the basin or aquifer scope.
- Collect hydrological data: flows, withdrawals, scarcity indices.
- Map allocation regimes: treaties, customary law, unilateral infrastructure.
- Analyze infrastructure disputes: dams, diversions, environmental impact.
- Review conflict and cooperation history.
- Assess future risk under climate and development scenarios.
## 7. Data requirements

- UN Water and FAO AQUASTAT
- Transboundary river basin commission reports
- Treaty texts and protocols
- World Bank water data
- Think-tank water studies (SIWI, IISS)
- National water ministry data
## 8. Approved public sources

- UN Water
- FAO AQUASTAT
- World Bank
- SIWI
- IISS
- National water ministries
- Basin commissions
## 9. Source verification standards

- Hydrological data quality varies by country; mark estimates. Distinguish surface water from groundwater. Water
conflict is often latent rather than kinetic — do not overstate.
## 10. Analytical framework

- Water-security framework: map basins and treaties, assess scarcity and drivers, analyze infrastructure disputes, and evaluate conflict/cooperation dynamics under climate and development stress.
## 11. Evidence requirements

- Use the ANBU seven-label evidence vocabulary (FACT, VERIFIED, UNVERIFIED, ASSESSMENT, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS,
SPECULATION). Every claim must carry an evidence label. Load-bearing claims require corroboration. Inferences
must be logically derivable from cited evidence. Speculation must be explicitly labeled and confined to
clearly marked sections.
## 12. Confidence assessment

- Assess confidence on three dimensions: source confidence (trustworthiness of origin), information confidence
(reliability of the specific item), and analytical confidence (soundness of the inference). Report all three.
Use HIGH (multiple corroborated sources, strong analytical basis), MODERATE (single reliable source or limited
corroboration), LOW (weak sourcing or significant analytical uncertainty), INSUFFICIENT (inadequate evidence
to support assessment).
## 13. Common analytical mistakes

- Treating water scarcity as deterministic conflict driver.
- Ignoring groundwater (often the real battleground).
- Assuming upstream control equals downstream vulnerability without accounting for alternatives.
- Missing cooperation mechanisms that have worked historically.
## 14. Ethical considerations

- Analysis only; no assistance in water infrastructure sabotage or diversion.
## 15. Security boundaries

- Public sources only. Do not facilitate attacks on water infrastructure or weaponization of water resources.
## 16. Expected output

- A water-security brief: basin map, scarcity assessment, infrastructure disputes, conflict/cooperation
analysis, confidence labels.
## 17. Quality checklist

- Basin defined
- Hydrology mapped
- Allocation regimes assessed
- Infrastructure disputes cataloged
- Conflict/cooperation history reviewed
- Confidence graded
