# International Organization Analysis
**Division:** Geopolitical | **Type:** Analytical Reference | **Version:** 1.0
## 1. Purpose

- Evaluate the structure, mandate, funding, governance, and strategic performance of international organizations relevant to geopolitical questions.
## 2. Scope

- Universal and regional international organizations: UN system, Bretton Woods institutions, security organizations, and functional agencies. Treaties and regimes not embodied in organizations are covered under legal/ and here only where an org implements them.
## 3. Intelligence objectives

- Map an organization's mandate, membership, governance, and funding structure.
- Assess its strategic relevance to a given geopolitical question.
- Evaluate performance: agenda-setting, implementation, enforcement, legitimacy.
- Identify reform pressures and likely trajectories.
## 4. Core concepts

- Principal-agent problems in IOs
- Weighted voting and veto politics
- Legitimacy and compliance
- Functional vs universal membership
## 5. Analytical methodology

- Institutional-performance framework: establish mandate and design, map governance and finance, review outputs and outcomes, assess legitimacy and compliance pull, and forecast reform or decline trajectories.
## 6. Step-by-step workflow

- Identify the organization and the analytical question it bears on.
- Extract mandate, membership rules, decision procedures, and budget from official documents.
- Review outputs: resolutions, programs, reports, enforcement actions.
- Assess outcomes: implementation rates, compliance records, impact evaluations.
- Evaluate legitimacy: member satisfaction, regional representation, reform demands.
- Grade performance and forecast trajectory.
## 7. Data requirements

- Organization charters and constitutions
- Official resolutions, reports, and budget documents
- Independent evaluation reports (e.g., OECD, academic)
- Think-tank institutional assessments
- News coverage of governance disputes
- Academic institutionalist literature
## 8. Approved public sources

- UN
- World Bank
- IMF
- WTO
- Regional organizations
- CSIS
- Chatham House
- Academic IR literature
## 9. Source verification standards

- Official documents define mandate; outcomes require independent evaluation. Do not equate activity
(resolutions passed) with effectiveness (behavior changed).
## 10. Analytical framework

- Institutional-performance framework: establish mandate and design, map governance and finance, review outputs and outcomes, assess legitimacy and compliance pull, and forecast reform or decline trajectories.
## 11. Evidence requirements

- Use the ANBU seven-label evidence vocabulary (FACT, VERIFIED, UNVERIFIED, ASSESSMENT, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS,
SPECULATION). Every claim must carry an evidence label. Load-bearing claims require corroboration. Inferences
must be logically derivable from cited evidence. Speculation must be explicitly labeled and confined to
clearly marked sections.
## 12. Confidence assessment

- Assess confidence on three dimensions: source confidence (trustworthiness of origin), information confidence
(reliability of the specific item), and analytical confidence (soundness of the inference). Report all three.
Use HIGH (multiple corroborated sources, strong analytical basis), MODERATE (single reliable source or limited
corroboration), LOW (weak sourcing or significant analytical uncertainty), INSUFFICIENT (inadequate evidence
to support assessment).
## 13. Common analytical mistakes

- Judging effectiveness by output volume rather than outcomes.
- Ignoring the principal-agent gap between member states and secretariats.
- Overlooking financing as a source of influence.
- Treating organizational decline narratives without checking usage data.
## 14. Ethical considerations

- Analysis only; no advice on covert influence over organizations.
## 15. Security boundaries

- Public sources only. No assistance in bribing officials, leaking documents, or covert lobbying.
## 16. Expected output

- An organizational dossier: design map, governance analysis, performance grade, legitimacy assessment,
trajectory forecast.
## 17. Quality checklist

- Mandate and design documented
- Governance and funding mapped
- Outputs vs outcomes distinguished
- Legitimacy assessed
- Trajectory forecast with confidence
- Neutral tone
