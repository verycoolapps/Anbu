# National Budget Tracking

State-domain intelligence file for the ANBU analytical repository. Focus: **national budget tracking** under Indonesia's constitutional order (UUD 1945) and its democratic institutions.

## Purpose

Track Indonesia's national budget (APBN) year by year: revenue, spending, deficit, debt, and key policy changes. This file supports analysts monitoring fiscal policy evolution and budget priorities.

## Scope

Covers: APBN for each fiscal year; 'UU APBN' (budget law); 'perubahan APBN' (budget revisions); 'penerimaan negara' sources (tax, non-tax, grants); 'belanja negara' categories; 'defisit' targets; 'utang' management; 'kebijakan fiskal' (fiscal policy); 'APBN-P' (revised budget).

## Intelligence objectives

Enable analysts to (1) track APBN evolution, (2) identify policy shifts, (3) assess fiscal sustainability, (4) monitor debt levels, and (5) predict budget trends.

## Core concepts

APBN = national budget; 'UU APBN' = budget law; 'APBN-P' = revised budget; 'penerimaan negara' = revenue; 'belanja negara' = spending; 'defisit' = deficit; 'utang' = debt; 'kebijakan fiskal' = fiscal policy.

## Analytical methodology

Use Kemenkeu APBN documents; track 'UU APBN' amendments; analyze 'Laporan Keuangan' for execution; monitor debt statistics; examine 'LKPP' reports.

## Step-by-step workflow

Step 1: identify APBN for year. Step 2: analyze revenue/spending. Step 3: track revisions. Step 4: assess sustainability. Step 5: predict trends.

## Data requirements

APBN documents; 'UU APBN' text; 'Laporan Keuangan' reports; debt statistics; 'LKPP' reports; 'perubahan APBN' records.

## Approved public sources

kemenkeu.go.id; bpk.go.id; dpr.go.id (budget deliberation); bps.go.id. Verify APBN data via official Kemenkeu/DPR sources.

## Source verification standards

Cross-check APBN figures with Kemenkeu; verify 'UU APBN' with official text; confirm debt data with official statistics.

## Analytical framework

APBN tracking model: revenue → spending → deficit → debt → sustainability. Identify trends and risks.

## Evidence requirements

APBN documents, budget laws, financial reports, debt statistics, performance reports.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH for official APBN/Kemenkeu data. MODERATE for trend assessments. LOW for speculative fiscal predictions.

## Common analytical mistakes

Confusing APBN with APBN-P; ignoring 'perubahan APBN'; treating budget as immutable; missing 'utang' growth; relying on media budget summaries.

## Ethical considerations

Budget data is public. Avoid political bias; stick to official evidence.

## Security boundaries

Do not fabricate APBN figures or invent 'budget fraud' without audit evidence.

## Expected output

APBN tracking memos with year-by-year analysis, policy shifts, sustainability assessment, and trend prediction. Cite Kemenkeu/DPR data.

## Quality checklist

APBN identified? Revenue/spending analyzed? Revisions tracked? Sustainability assessed? Trends predicted?
