# Government Structure Analysis

State-domain intelligence file for the ANBU analytical repository. Focus: **government structure analysis** under Indonesia's constitutional order (UUD 1945) and its democratic institutions.

## Purpose

Analyze the formal and informal structure of the Indonesian government: how power flows from President to ministries, how DPR oversees, how MK reviews, and how regional governments fit. This file supports analysts examining executive decision-making, legislative-executive relations, and inter-branch conflict.

## Scope

Covers: Presidential system under UUD 1945; Cabinet (Kabinet) composition and functions; DPR/DPP/DPD roles; separation of powers; checks and balances; regional autonomy (otonomi daerah) under Law 23/2014; vertical and horizontal coordination mechanisms. Does NOT cover foreign governments or detailed local politics unless affecting national structure.

## Intelligence objectives

Enable analysts to (1) trace how a policy moves from proposal to implementation, (2) identify veto points, (3) assess executive-legislative tension, (4) understand regional-central relations, and (5) predict impact of institutional reforms.

## Core concepts

Presidential system (not parliamentary); Cabinet as executive collective; DPR as legislature with budget and oversight powers; MK as constitutional court; 'otonomi daerah' (regional autonomy); 'desentralisasi' vs. 'dekonsentrasi'; 'pembangunan' (development) as state function.

## Analytical methodology

Read Perpres on cabinet formation; analyze DPR plenary sessions; track MK decisions on executive-legislative disputes; examine Law 23/2014 on regional government; monitor Bappenas development plans.

## Step-by-step workflow

Step 1: map formal structure from UUD and laws. Step 2: identify informal power centers (e.g., palace, party leaders). Step 3: trace recent policy decisions through branches. Step 4: assess coordination mechanisms. Step 5: note tensions or conflicts.

## Data requirements

Perpres on cabinet; UU on DPR, DPD, MPR; UU 23/2014 on regional government; Bappenas RPJMN (national development plan); DPR budget deliberation records; MK decisions on institutional disputes.

## Approved public sources

perpres.go.id; dpr.go.id; dpd.go.id; mpr.go.id; mkri.id; bappenas.go.id; kemendagri.go.id (home affairs). Verify 'Kabinet' composition via official Perpres, not news.

## Source verification standards

Cross-check structural claims against UUD 1945 and implementing laws; verify current cabinet via latest Perpres; confirm regional autonomy framework under Law 23/2014.

## Analytical framework

Three-branch model: Executive (President + Cabinet) → Legislative (DPR/DPD/MPR) → Judicial (MA/MK/KY). Overlay regional layer (provinsi, kabupaten/kota) under central supervision.

## Evidence requirements

Cabinet Perpres, law texts, MK decisions on institutional disputes, development plan documents.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH for formal structure under UUD and laws. MODERATE for informal power dynamics. LOW for speculative 'shadow governance'.

## Common analytical mistakes

Treating Indonesia as parliamentary (it's presidential); ignoring DPD's limited role; assuming regional governments are independent (they are autonomous but under central supervision); missing 'Lembaga' that are not ministries.

## Ethical considerations

Government structure is public information. Avoid speculation about 'real power' without evidence; stick to formal and documented informal roles.

## Security boundaries

No classified content. Do not fabricate cabinet members or invent 'parallel structures'.

## Expected output

Structural memos with formal/informal maps, policy flow diagrams, and tension points. Cite UUD, laws, and Perpres.

## Quality checklist

Branches correctly identified? Cabinet current? Regional framework cited? Informal roles documented? Conflicts noted?
