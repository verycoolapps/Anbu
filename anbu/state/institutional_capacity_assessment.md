# Institutional Capacity Assessment

State-domain intelligence file for the ANBU analytical repository. Focus: **institutional capacity assessment** under Indonesia's constitutional order (UUD 1945) and its democratic institutions.

## Purpose

Assess Indonesia's institutional capacity: human resources, financial resources, technical capacity, and organizational effectiveness. This file supports analysts examining 'kapasitas institusi' and reform needs.

## Scope

Covers: 'kapasitas institusi' (institutional capacity); 'SDM' (human resources); 'ASN' capacity; 'anggaran' (budget) capacity; 'teknologi' (technology) capacity; 'organisasi' (organization) effectiveness; 'reformasi birokrasi' (bureaucratic reform); 'LKPP' performance; 'BPK' audit findings.

## Intelligence objectives

Enable analysts to (1) assess institutional capacity, (2) identify weaknesses, (3) evaluate reform progress, (4) recommend improvements, and (5) predict capacity outcomes.

## Core concepts

Kapasitas = capacity; 'SDM' = human resources; 'ASN' = civil servants; 'anggaran' = budget; 'teknologi' = technology; 'organisasi' = organization; 'reformasi' = reform; 'efektivitas' = effectiveness.

## Analytical methodology

Use BKN data for HR; analyze ministry budgets; assess technology adoption; evaluate organizational structures; monitor 'reformasi birokrasi' programs; examine LKPP/BPK reports.

## Step-by-step workflow

Step 1: assess HR capacity. Step 2: evaluate financial capacity. Step 3: analyze technical capacity. Step 4: assess organizational effectiveness. Step 5: recommend improvements.

## Data requirements

BKN statistics; ministry budget data; technology adoption reports; organizational charts; 'reformasi birokrasi' reports; LKPP/BPK findings.

## Approved public sources

bkn.go.id; kemenkeu.go.id; each ministry's 'SDM' page; bpk.go.id; bappenas.go.id. Verify capacity data via official sources.

## Source verification standards

Cross-check HR data with BKN; verify budget data with Kemenkeu; confirm effectiveness with LKPP/BPK.

## Analytical framework

Capacity model: HR → financial → technical → organizational → effectiveness. Identify weak links.

## Evidence requirements

BKN data, budget figures, technology reports, organizational charts, reform reports, audit findings.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH for official BKN/Kemenkeu data. MODERATE for effectiveness assessments. LOW for speculative capacity claims.

## Common analytical mistakes

Confusing capacity with performance; ignoring regional variation; treating 'reformasi' as complete; missing informal capacity; relying on ministry self-assessments.

## Ethical considerations

Capacity data is public. Avoid political bias; stick to official evidence.

## Security boundaries

Do not fabricate capacity data or invent 'incompetent institutions' without official evidence.

## Expected output

Capacity memos with HR/financial/technical/organizational assessment, weakness identification, and improvement recommendations. Cite BKN/Kemenkeu/LKPP data.

## Quality checklist

HR assessed? Financial evaluated? Technical analyzed? Organizational assessed? Recommendations evidence-based?
