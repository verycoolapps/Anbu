# Regulatory Intelligence

State-domain intelligence file for the ANBU analytical repository. Focus: **regulatory intelligence** under Indonesia's constitutional order (UUD 1945) and its democratic institutions.

## Purpose

Track and analyze Indonesia's regulatory framework: laws, regulations, and their impact on business, society, and governance. This file supports analysts monitoring 'peraturan' changes and regulatory risks.

## Scope

Covers: 'peraturan perundang-undangan' (legislation) hierarchy: UUD 1945 > UU (laws) > PP (government regulations) > Perpres > Permen (ministerial) > Perda (local); 'peraturan.go.id' database; 'Rancangan Peraturan' (draft regulations); 'omnibus law' (Cipta Kerja); 'regulasi' impact assessment; 'harmonisasi' (harmonization) process.

## Intelligence objectives

Enable analysts to (1) track regulatory changes, (2) assess impacts, (3) identify risks, (4) compare regulations, and (5) predict regulatory trends.

## Core concepts

Peraturan = regulation; 'perundang-undangan' = legislation; 'omnibus law' = omnibus law; 'harmonisasi' = harmonization; 'regulasi' = regulation; 'UU' = law; 'PP' = government regulation; 'Perpres' = presidential regulation; 'Permen' = ministerial regulation; 'Perda' = local regulation.

## Analytical methodology

Use peraturan.go.id; track 'Rancangan Peraturan'; analyze 'omnibus law' impacts; examine 'harmonisasi' processes; monitor 'regulasi' changes; assess 'impact assessment' reports.

## Step-by-step workflow

Step 1: identify regulation. Step 2: trace formulation. Step 3: assess impact. Step 4: compare with existing. Step 5: predict trends.

## Data requirements

peraturan.go.id database; 'Rancangan Peraturan' drafts; 'omnibus law' texts; 'harmonisasi' reports; impact assessment studies; 'LKPP' reports.

## Approved public sources

peraturan.go.id; kemenkumham.go.id (legal affairs); dpr.go.id (legislation); bpk.go.id. Verify regulations via official database, not news summaries.

## Source verification standards

Cross-check regulations with peraturan.go.id; verify 'Rancangan Peraturan' with official drafts; confirm impacts with official studies.

## Analytical framework

Regulatory hierarchy model: UUD → UU → PP → Perpres → Permen → Perda. Map formulation and impact.

## Evidence requirements

Regulation texts, draft documents, impact assessments, harmonization reports, performance reports.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH for official peraturan.go.id data. MODERATE for impact assessments. LOW for speculative regulatory predictions.

## Common analytical mistakes

Confusing UU with PP; ignoring 'omnibus law' complexity; treating 'Perda' as national; missing 'harmonisasi' conflicts; relying on media regulatory summaries.

## Ethical considerations

Regulatory data is public. Avoid political bias; stick to official evidence.

## Security boundaries

Do not fabricate regulations or invent 'secret laws' without official text.

## Expected output

Regulatory memos with change tracking, impact assessment, risk identification, and trend prediction. Cite peraturan.go.id data.

## Quality checklist

Regulation identified? Formulation traced? Impact assessed? Conflicts noted? Trends predicted?
