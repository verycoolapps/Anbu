# Public Procurement Transparency

State-domain intelligence file for the ANBU analytical repository. Focus: **public procurement transparency** under Indonesia's constitutional order (UUD 1945) and its democratic institutions.

## Purpose

Analyze Indonesia's public procurement system: transparency, corruption risks, efficiency, and reform. This file supports analysts examining 'pengadaan barang/jasa pemerintah' and 'e-katalog' system.

## Scope

Covers: 'pengadaan' (procurement) of goods/services; 'LPSE' (Layanan Pengadaan Secara Elektronik); 'e-katalog' (e-catalog); 'lelang' (tender); 'Swakelola' (self-management); 'K/L' procurement; 'Pemda' procurement; 'LKPP' role; 'e-procurement' system; corruption in procurement; 'mark-up' practices.

## Intelligence objectives

Enable analysts to (1) understand procurement system, (2) assess transparency, (3) identify corruption risks, (4) evaluate efficiency, and (5) recommend improvements.

## Core concepts

Pengadaan = procurement; 'LPSE' = electronic procurement service; 'e-katalog' = e-catalog; 'lelang' = tender; 'Swakelola' = self-management; 'mark-up' = price markup; 'KKN' = corruption, collusion, nepotism; 'efisiensi' = efficiency.

## Analytical methodology

Use LPSE data; analyze 'e-katalog' prices; track 'lelang' results; examine KPK procurement corruption cases; monitor 'LKPP' reports; assess 'Swakelola' usage.

## Step-by-step workflow

Step 1: map procurement system. Step 2: assess transparency. Step 3: identify corruption risks. Step 4: evaluate efficiency. Step 5: recommend improvements.

## Data requirements

LPSE data; 'e-katalog' prices; 'lelang' results; KPK procurement cases; 'LKPP' reports; 'Swakelola' statistics.

## Approved public sources

lpse.lkpp.go.id; kpk.go.id; bpk.go.id; each ministry's 'pengadaan' page. Verify procurement data via official LPSE/LKPP sources.

## Source verification standards

Cross-check procurement figures with LPSE; verify corruption cases with KPK; confirm efficiency with 'LKPP' reports.

## Analytical framework

Procurement model: need → tender → selection → contract → delivery → audit. Identify weak links.

## Evidence requirements

LPSE data, e-catalog prices, tender results, KPK cases, LKPP reports.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH for official LPSE/LKPP data. MODERATE for corruption risk assessments. LOW for speculative 'mark-up' claims.

## Common analytical mistakes

Assuming all procurement is corrupt; ignoring 'Swakelola' legitimate uses; treating 'e-katalog' as always cheaper; missing regional variation; relying on media corruption stories.

## Ethical considerations

Procurement data is public. Avoid defamation when discussing corruption; stick to official KPK/BPK findings.

## Security boundaries

Do not fabricate procurement data or invent 'corrupt vendors' without official findings.

## Expected output

Procurement memos with system analysis, transparency assessment, corruption risk map, and improvement recommendations. Cite LPSE/LKPP/KPK data.

## Quality checklist

System mapped? Transparency assessed? Risks identified? Efficiency evaluated? Recommendations evidence-based?
