# Government Accountability

State-domain intelligence file for the ANBU analytical repository. Focus: **government accountability** under Indonesia's constitutional order (UUD 1945) and its democratic institutions.

## Purpose

Analyze Indonesia's government accountability: oversight mechanisms, anti-corruption efforts, transparency, and public trust. This file supports analysts examining 'akuntabilitas pemerintah' and 'pengawasan'.

## Scope

Covers: 'akuntabilitas' (accountability); 'pengawasan' (oversight); 'BPK' (audit board); 'BPKP' (government internal audit); 'KPK' (corruption eradication); 'DPR' oversight; 'MK' judicial review; 'LKPP' reports; 'transparansi' (transparency); 'partisipasi' (participation); 'whistleblower' protection.

## Intelligence objectives

Enable analysts to (1) assess accountability mechanisms, (2) evaluate oversight effectiveness, (3) analyze transparency, (4) measure public trust, and (5) recommend improvements.

## Core concepts

Akuntabilitas = accountability; 'pengawasan' = oversight; 'BPK' = audit board; 'BPKP' = internal audit; 'KPK' = corruption commission; 'MK' = constitutional court; 'LKPP' = performance report; 'transparansi' = transparency; 'whistleblower' = pelapor.

## Analytical methodology

Use BPK/BPKP audit reports; analyze KPK cases; track DPR oversight; examine MK decisions; assess LKPP reports; monitor transparency initiatives; evaluate whistleblower protection.

## Step-by-step workflow

Step 1: map oversight mechanisms. Step 2: assess effectiveness. Step 3: analyze transparency. Step 4: measure trust. Step 5: recommend improvements.

## Data requirements

BPK/BPKP audit reports; KPK case data; DPR oversight records; MK decisions; LKPP reports; transparency data; whistleblower statistics.

## Approved public sources

bpk.go.id; bpkp.go.id; kpk.go.id; dpr.go.id; mkri.id; bappenas.go.id. Verify accountability data via official sources.

## Source verification standards

Cross-check audit findings with BPK/BPKP; verify corruption cases with KPK; confirm oversight with DPR records.

## Analytical framework

Accountability model: oversight → audit → prosecution → transparency → trust. Identify weak links.

## Evidence requirements

Audit reports, corruption cases, oversight records, court decisions, performance reports, transparency data.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH for official BPK/KPK/DPR data. MODERATE for trust assessments. LOW for speculative accountability claims.

## Common analytical mistakes

Confusing oversight with accountability; ignoring 'BPKP' role; treating 'KPK' as only anti-corruption; missing informal accountability; relying on media corruption stories.

## Ethical considerations

Accountability data is public. Avoid political bias; stick to official evidence.

## Security boundaries

Do not fabricate accountability data or invent 'corrupt officials' without official findings.

## Expected output

Accountability memos with oversight assessment, transparency analysis, trust measurement, and improvement recommendations. Cite BPK/KPK/DPR data.

## Quality checklist

Oversight mapped? Effectiveness assessed? Transparency analyzed? Trust measured? Recommendations evidence-based?
