# Regional Government Analysis

State-domain intelligence file for the ANBU analytical repository. Focus: **regional government analysis** under Indonesia's constitutional order (UUD 1945) and its democratic institutions.

## Purpose

Analyze Indonesia's regional governments: provinces, regencies/cities, their autonomy, functions, and relations with central government. This file supports analysts examining decentralization, regional development, and center-region relations.

## Scope

Covers: 34 provinces; 416 regencies/cities (kabupaten/kota); 'pemda' = pemerintah daerah (regional government); 'otonomi daerah' (regional autonomy); 'urusan' (government affairs) under Law 23/2014; 'perimbangan keuangan' (financial balance); 'pemilihan kepala daerah' (pilkada); 'perda' (local regulations).

## Intelligence objectives

Enable analysts to (1) understand regional government structure, (2) assess autonomy implementation, (3) track regional development, (4) identify center-region tensions, and (5) evaluate regional performance.

## Core concepts

Pemda = regional government; 'otonomi daerah' = regional autonomy; 'urusan' = government affairs; 'perimbangan keuangan' = financial balance; 'pilkada' = regional elections; 'perda' = local regulations; 'desentralisasi' = decentralization; 'dekonsentrasi' = deconcentration.

## Analytical methodology

Use Kemendagri data; analyze Perda; track 'pilkada' results; examine 'Laporan Kinerja' of pemda; monitor 'perimbangan keuangan'; analyze BPS regional statistics.

## Step-by-step workflow

Step 1: map regional government structure. Step 2: assess autonomy implementation. Step 3: track regional development. Step 4: identify tensions. Step 5: evaluate performance.

## Data requirements

Kemendagri statistics; Perda database; 'pilkada' results; pemda performance reports; BPS regional data; 'perimbangan keuangan' allocations.

## Approved public sources

kemendagri.go.id; each pemda's official site; bps.go.id; kpu.go.id (pilkada); bpk.go.id (audit). Verify pemda data via official sources, not local news.

## Source verification standards

Cross-check pemda statistics with Kemendagri; verify Perda with official database; confirm 'pilkada' results with KPU.

## Analytical framework

Regional autonomy model: central → provincial → regency/city. Map 'urusan' distribution and 'perimbangan keuangan'.

## Evidence requirements

Kemendagri data, Perda, pilkada results, pemda reports, BPS statistics, audit findings.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH for official Kemendagri/KPU data. MODERATE for autonomy assessments. LOW for anecdotal regional issues.

## Common analytical mistakes

Assuming all pemda have same autonomy; ignoring 'urusan' distribution; treating 'perda' as always valid; missing urban-rural variation; relying on Jakarta-centric views.

## Ethical considerations

Regional government data is public. Avoid regional discrimination; stick to official findings.

## Security boundaries

Do not fabricate pemda data or invent 'corrupt regions' without audit evidence.

## Expected output

Regional government memos with structure analysis, autonomy assessment, and performance evaluation. Cite Kemendagri/KPU data.

## Quality checklist

Pemda structure mapped? Autonomy assessed? Development tracked? Tensions identified? Performance evaluated?
