# Intergovernmental Relations

State-domain intelligence file for the ANBU analytical repository. Focus: **intergovernmental relations** under Indonesia's constitutional order (UUD 1945) and its democratic institutions.

## Purpose

Analyze relations between central and regional governments in Indonesia: coordination, conflict, fiscal relations, and political dynamics. This file supports analysts examining 'hubungan pemerintah pusat dan daerah' and center-region tensions.

## Scope

Covers: central-regional coordination mechanisms; 'perimbangan keuangan' (fiscal balance); 'otonomi daerah' implementation; 'perda' review; 'pilkada' dynamics; 'otonomi khusus' relations; 'kewenangan' (authority) disputes; 'pengawasan' (oversight) by central government.

## Intelligence objectives

Enable analysts to (1) understand center-region relations, (2) identify conflict points, (3) assess coordination mechanisms, (4) evaluate fiscal relations, and (5) predict intergovernmental tensions.

## Core concepts

Intergovernmental relations = 'hubungan antar pemerintah'; 'perimbangan keuangan' = fiscal balance; 'otonomi daerah' = regional autonomy; 'perda' = local regulations; 'pengawasan' = oversight; 'kewenangan' = authority; 'koordinasi' = coordination.

## Analytical methodology

Use Kemendagri coordination data; analyze 'perimbangan keuangan' disputes; track 'perda' review; examine 'pilkada' dynamics; monitor 'otonomi khusus' relations; assess MK decisions on central-regional disputes.

## Step-by-step workflow

Step 1: map coordination mechanisms. Step 2: identify conflict points. Step 3: assess fiscal relations. Step 4: evaluate political dynamics. Step 5: predict tensions.

## Data requirements

Kemendagri coordination reports; 'perimbangan keuangan' data; 'perda' review records; 'pilkada' results; MK decisions on intergovernmental disputes.

## Approved public sources

kemendagri.go.id; kemenkeu.go.id (fiscal); mkri.id (disputes); kpu.go.id (pilkada); bps.go.id. Verify intergovernmental data via official sources.

## Source verification standards

Cross-check coordination mechanisms with Kemendagri; verify fiscal disputes with official allocations; confirm MK decisions with official records.

## Analytical framework

Intergovernmental relations model: coordination → fiscal → political → conflict → resolution. Identify weak links.

## Evidence requirements

Kemendagri reports, fiscal data, Perda records, pilkada results, MK decisions.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH for official coordination/fiscal data. MODERATE for conflict assessments. LOW for speculative political tensions.

## Common analytical mistakes

Assuming center-region relations are harmonious; ignoring fiscal constraints; treating 'otonomi khusus' as separate; missing informal politics; relying on Jakarta-centric views.

## Ethical considerations

Intergovernmental data is public. Avoid political bias; stick to official evidence.

## Security boundaries

Do not fabricate intergovernmental disputes or invent 'separatist movements' without evidence.

## Expected output

Intergovernmental memos with coordination analysis, conflict assessment, and tension prediction. Cite Kemendagri/MK data.

## Quality checklist

Coordination mapped? Conflicts identified? Fiscal relations assessed? Political dynamics evaluated? Tensions predicted?
