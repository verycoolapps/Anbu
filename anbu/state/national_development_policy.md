# National Development Policy

State-domain intelligence file for the ANBU analytical repository. Focus: **national development policy** under Indonesia's constitutional order (UUD 1945) and its democratic institutions.

## Purpose

Analyze Indonesia's national development policy: RPJMN, development priorities, sectoral plans, and implementation. This file supports analysts examining long-term development strategy, resource allocation, and development outcomes.

## Scope

Covers: RPJMN (Rencana Pembangunan Jangka Menengah Nasional) 2020-2024 and 2025-2029; sectoral development plans; ' visi misi' of presidents; Bappenas role; development indicators (IPM, kemiskinan, ketimpangan); regional development plans (RPJMD); 'pembangunan' vs. 'pertumbuhan' distinction.

## Intelligence objectives

Enable analysts to (1) understand national development priorities, (2) assess progress toward targets, (3) evaluate sectoral performance, (4) identify development gaps, and (5) predict development outcomes.

## Core concepts

RPJMN = national medium-term development plan; 'visi misi' = vision mission; 'pembangunan' = development; 'pertumbuhan' = growth; 'IPM' = Indeks Pembangunan Manusia (HDI); 'kemiskinan' = poverty; 'ketimpangan' = inequality; 'Bappenas' = planning agency.

## Analytical methodology

Use Bappenas RPJMN documents; track development indicators via BPS; analyze sectoral plans; monitor ministry 'Rencana Strategis'; examine 'Laporan Kinerja' for progress.

## Step-by-step workflow

Step 1: identify RPJMN priorities. Step 2: map to sectoral plans. Step 3: track indicators. Step 4: assess progress. Step 5: identify gaps.

## Data requirements

RPJMN documents; BPS development statistics; ministry strategic plans; 'Laporan Kinerja' reports; BPK audit findings on development projects.

## Approved public sources

bappenas.go.id; bps.go.id; each ministry's 'renstra' page; bpk.go.id. Verify development indicators via BPS, not ministry claims.

## Source verification standards

Cross-check RPJMN targets with BPS data; verify sectoral progress with ministry reports; confirm audit findings with BPK.

## Analytical framework

Development planning model: vision → strategy → programs → indicators → evaluation. Identify weak links.

## Evidence requirements

RPJMN documents, BPS statistics, ministry plans, performance reports, audit findings.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH for official plans and BPS data. MODERATE for progress assessments. LOW for speculative development predictions.

## Common analytical mistakes

Confusing growth with development; ignoring distributional effects; treating RPJMN as binding (it's a plan); missing regional variations; relying on ministry self-assessments.

## Ethical considerations

Development data is public. Avoid political bias when evaluating development outcomes; stick to BPS/BPK evidence.

## Security boundaries

Do not fabricate development statistics or invent 'failed projects' without audit evidence.

## Expected output

Development policy memos with priority analysis, progress assessment, and gap identification. Cite RPJMN/BPS data.

## Quality checklist

RPJMN priorities identified? Sectoral plans mapped? Indicators tracked? Progress assessed? Gaps documented?
