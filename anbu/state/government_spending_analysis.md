# Government Spending Analysis

State-domain intelligence file for the ANBU analytical repository. Focus: **government spending analysis** under Indonesia's constitutional order (UUD 1945) and its democratic institutions.

## Purpose

Analyze Indonesia's government spending: where money goes, spending efficiency, corruption risks, and policy effectiveness. This file supports analysts examining 'belanja negara' priorities and outcomes.

## Scope

Covers: 'belanja negara' categories (personnel, goods/services, capital, transfers, social); 'belanja pegawai' (personnel); 'belanja modal' (capital/infrastructure); 'subsidi' (subsidies); 'transfer ke daerah'; 'belanja bantuan sosial' (social assistance); 'belanja riset' (R&D); spending efficiency; 'value for money' assessment.

## Intelligence objectives

Enable analysts to (1) map spending priorities, (2) assess efficiency, (3) identify corruption risks, (4) evaluate outcomes, and (5) recommend improvements.

## Core concepts

Belanja negara = state spending; 'belanja pegawai' = personnel; 'belanja modal' = capital; 'subsidi' = subsidies; 'transfer ke daerah' = transfers; 'bansos' = social assistance; 'efisiensi' = efficiency; 'value for money' = VfM.

## Analytical methodology

Use Kemenkeu spending data; analyze 'Laporan Keuangan' by category; track 'LKPP' for outcomes; examine BPK audit findings on spending; monitor 'subsidi' effectiveness.

## Step-by-step workflow

Step 1: map spending categories. Step 2: assess priorities. Step 3: evaluate efficiency. Step 4: identify risks. Step 5: recommend improvements.

## Data requirements

Kemenkeu spending data; 'Laporan Keuangan' reports; 'LKPP' reports; BPK audit findings; 'subsidi' effectiveness studies.

## Approved public sources

kemenkeu.go.id; bpk.go.id; bappenas.go.id; bps.go.id. Verify spending data via official Kemenkeu/BPK sources.

## Source verification standards

Cross-check spending figures with Kemenkeu; verify efficiency with audit findings; confirm outcomes with 'LKPP' reports.

## Analytical framework

Spending analysis model: allocation → execution → outcome → efficiency → accountability. Identify weak links.

## Evidence requirements

Spending data, financial reports, performance reports, audit findings, effectiveness studies.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH for official Kemenkeu/BPK data. MODERATE for efficiency assessments. LOW for speculative corruption claims.

## Common analytical mistakes

Confusing spending with budget; ignoring 'belanja pegawai' rigidity; treating 'subsidi' as always efficient; missing regional variation; relying on media spending summaries.

## Ethical considerations

Spending data is public. Avoid political bias; stick to official evidence.

## Security boundaries

Do not fabricate spending figures or invent 'spending corruption' without audit evidence.

## Expected output

Spending analysis memos with priority mapping, efficiency assessment, risk identification, and improvement recommendations. Cite Kemenkeu/BPK data.

## Quality checklist

Spending mapped? Priorities assessed? Efficiency evaluated? Risks identified? Recommendations evidence-based?
