# Public Audit Analysis

State-domain intelligence file for the ANBU analytical repository. Focus: **public audit analysis** under Indonesia's constitutional order (UUD 1945) and its democratic institutions.

## Purpose

Analyze Indonesia's public audit system: BPK (audit board), BPKP (internal audit), and their findings on government performance, financial management, and compliance. This file supports analysts examining 'audit' and 'pengawasan keuangan'.

## Scope

Covers: 'BPK' (Badan Pemeriksa Keuangan) external audit; 'BPKP' (Badan Pengawasan Keuangan dan Pembangunan) internal audit; 'audit' types (keuangan, kinerja, kepatuhan); 'temuan audit' (audit findings); 'LKTT' (annual financial reports); 'LKjPP' (performance reports); 'opini audit' (audit opinion); 'perbaikan' (correction) process.

## Intelligence objectives

Enable analysts to (1) understand audit system, (2) analyze findings, (3) assess compliance, (4) evaluate performance, and (5) recommend improvements.

## Core concepts

BPK = external audit board; BPKP = internal audit; 'audit' = audit; 'temuan' = finding; 'LKTT' = annual financial report; 'LKjPP' = performance report; 'opini' = opinion; 'perbaikan' = correction; 'kepatuhan' = compliance.

## Analytical methodology

Use BPK/BPKP audit reports; analyze 'temuan audit'; track 'LKTT'/'LKjPP'; assess 'opini audit'; monitor 'perbaikan' process; evaluate compliance.

## Step-by-step workflow

Step 1: identify audit reports. Step 2: analyze findings. Step 3: assess compliance. Step 4: evaluate performance. Step 5: recommend improvements.

## Data requirements

BPK/BPKP audit reports; 'temuan audit' data; 'LKTT'/'LKjPP' reports; 'opini audit' records; 'perbaikan' data.

## Approved public sources

bpk.go.id; bpkp.go.id; kemenkeu.go.id; each ministry's 'audit' page. Verify audit data via official sources.

## Source verification standards

Cross-check findings with BPK/BPKP; verify opinions with official records; confirm corrections with official data.

## Analytical framework

Audit model: planning → execution → reporting → follow-up → improvement. Identify weak links.

## Evidence requirements

Audit reports, findings, financial reports, performance reports, opinions, correction data.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH for official BPK/BPKP data. MODERATE for compliance assessments. LOW for speculative audit claims.

## Common analytical mistakes

Confusing BPK with BPKP; ignoring 'temuan' significance; treating 'opini' as binary; missing 'perbaikan' gaps; relying on media audit summaries.

## Ethical considerations

Audit data is public. Avoid political bias; stick to official evidence.

## Security boundaries

Do not fabricate audit findings or invent 'audit fraud' without official evidence.

## Expected output

Audit memos with system analysis, findings assessment, compliance evaluation, performance analysis, and improvement recommendations. Cite BPK/BPKP data.

## Quality checklist

Reports identified? Findings analyzed? Compliance assessed? Performance evaluated? Recommendations evidence-based?
