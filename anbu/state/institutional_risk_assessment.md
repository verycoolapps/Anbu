# Institutional Risk Assessment

State-domain intelligence file for the ANBU analytical repository. Focus: **institutional risk assessment** under Indonesia's constitutional order (UUD 1945) and its democratic institutions.

## Purpose

Assess institutional risks in Indonesia: corruption, inefficiency, political interference, capacity gaps, and reform challenges. This file supports analysts identifying and mitigating 'risiko institusi'.

## Scope

Covers: 'risiko institusi' (institutional risk); 'korupsi' (corruption); 'inefisiensi' (inefficiency); 'intervensi politik' (political interference); 'kapasitas' (capacity) gaps; 'reformasi' (reform) challenges; 'bencana' (disaster) vulnerability; 'krisis' (crisis) preparedness; 'ketergantungan' (dependency) risks.

## Intelligence objectives

Enable analysts to (1) identify institutional risks, (2) assess severity, (3) evaluate mitigation, (4) prioritize risks, and (5) recommend improvements.

## Core concepts

Risiko = risk; 'korupsi' = corruption; 'inefisiensi' = inefficiency; 'intervensi' = interference; 'kapasitas' = capacity; 'reformasi' = reform; 'bencana' = disaster; 'krisis' = crisis; 'ketergantungan' = dependency.

## Analytical methodology

Use KPK/BPK data for corruption; analyze efficiency metrics; assess political interference; evaluate capacity gaps; monitor reform progress; examine disaster preparedness; identify dependencies.

## Step-by-step workflow

Step 1: identify risks. Step 2: assess severity. Step 3: evaluate mitigation. Step 4: prioritize. Step 5: recommend improvements.

## Data requirements

KPK/BPK data; efficiency metrics; political interference cases; capacity assessments; reform reports; disaster preparedness data; dependency analysis.

## Approved public sources

kpk.go.id; bpk.go.id; bpkp.go.id; each ministry's 'risiko' page; bnpb.go.id; bappenas.go.id. Verify risk data via official sources.

## Source verification standards

Cross-check corruption data with KPK/BPK; verify efficiency with official metrics; confirm risks with official assessments.

## Analytical framework

Risk model: identification → assessment → mitigation → monitoring → improvement. Prioritize high-severity risks.

## Evidence requirements

Corruption data, efficiency metrics, interference cases, capacity assessments, reform reports, disaster data.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH for official KPK/BPK data. MODERATE for risk assessments. LOW for speculative risk claims.

## Common analytical mistakes

Confusing risk with threat; ignoring 'mitigasi'; treating 'reformasi' as complete; missing 'bencana' risks; relying on media risk coverage.

## Ethical considerations

Risk data is public. Avoid political bias; stick to official evidence.

## Security boundaries

Do not fabricate risks or invent 'institutional collapse' without official evidence.

## Expected output

Risk memos with identification, severity assessment, mitigation evaluation, prioritization, and improvement recommendations. Cite KPK/BPK/ministry data.

## Quality checklist

Risks identified? Severity assessed? Mitigation evaluated? Prioritized? Recommendations evidence-based?
