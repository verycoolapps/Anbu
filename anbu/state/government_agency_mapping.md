# Government Agency Mapping

State-domain intelligence file for the ANBU analytical repository. Focus: **government agency mapping** under Indonesia's constitutional order (UUD 1945) and its democratic institutions.

## Purpose

Map Indonesia's non-ministerial government agencies (Lembaga) that are not ministries: independent institutions, coordinating bodies, and special agencies. This file complements ministry_directory.md by covering agencies like Bappenas, BKPM, NLC, and various Badan/Lembaga.

## Scope

Covers: Bappenas (National Development Planning Agency); BKPM (Investment Coordinating Board); NLC (National Logistics Committee); BPPT (Research and Technology); BRIN (National Research and Innovation Agency); LIPI (Indonesian Institute of Sciences); BPOM (Food and Drug Agency); BNN (National Narcotics Board); BASARNAS (Search and Rescue); BNPB (Disaster Management); Kementerian/Lembaga under Presidential coordination; and various 'Badan' under ministries.

## Intelligence objectives

Enable analysts to (1) identify non-ministerial agencies with specific mandates, (2) trace coordination between ministries and agencies, (3) assess institutional overlap, (4) track agency performance, and (5) predict impact of agency reorganization.

## Core concepts

Lembaga = non-ministerial agency; 'Badan' = agency; 'Lembaga' can be independent or under ministry; 'Koordinasi' (coordination) function; 'Pengawasan' (oversight) function; 'Pelayanan' (service) function.

## Analytical methodology

Use each agency's official website; cross-reference Perpres/UU on establishment; track agency heads via Presidential Decree; monitor agency strategic plans and performance reports.

## Step-by-step workflow

Step 1: list all Lembaga/Badan. Step 2: assign functions and reporting lines. Step 3: identify heads. Step 4: note coordination with ministries. Step 5: track recent performance.

## Data requirements

Perpres/UU on establishment; agency strategic plans; LKPP reports; BPK audit reports; news on agency reorganization.

## Approved public sources

Each agency's official site (bappenas.go.id, bkpm.go.id, bpt.go.id, etc.); perpres.go.id; bpk.go.id; bps.go.id. Verify agency heads via official decree.

## Source verification standards

Confirm agency still exists under current Perpres/UU; verify head name via official decree; check reporting lines against legal basis.

## Analytical framework

Classify by function: planning, coordination, research, oversight, service, emergency. Map reporting lines to President or minister.

## Evidence requirements

Perpres/UU on establishment, agency strategic plans, LKPP reports, BPK audit findings.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH for agencies with clear legal basis. MODERATE for newly created or recently reorganized agencies. LOW for informal 'task forces'.

## Common analytical mistakes

Treating all Lembaga as ministries; missing 'Badan' under ministries; assuming agencies are independent when they report to minister; ignoring 'Koordinasi' function.

## Ethical considerations

Agency information is public. Avoid speculation about agency corruption without official findings.

## Security boundaries

Do not fabricate agency names or invent 'secret agencies'. Verify Perpres before citing reorganization.

## Expected output

Agency directory with functions, heads, reporting lines, and recent performance. Include confidence per agency.

## Quality checklist

All agencies listed? Heads current? Reporting lines correct? Coordination noted? Performance reports cited?
