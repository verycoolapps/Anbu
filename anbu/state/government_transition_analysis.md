# Government Transition Analysis

State-domain intelligence file for the ANBU analytical repository. Focus: **government transition analysis** under Indonesia's constitutional order (UUD 1945) and its democratic institutions.

## Purpose

Analyze Indonesia's government transitions: presidential elections, cabinet changes, institutional continuity, and policy shifts during transitions. This file supports analysts examining 'transisi pemerintahan' and 'serah terima'.

## Scope

Covers: 'transisi pemerintahan' (government transition); 'pemilihan presiden' (presidential election); 'pelantikan' (inauguration); 'kabinet' formation; 'serah terima' (handover); 'kontinuitas' (continuity); 'perubahan kebijakan' (policy changes); 'instabilitas' (instability); 'transisi daerah' (regional transitions).

## Intelligence objectives

Enable analysts to (1) understand transition process, (2) assess continuity/change, (3) identify risks, (4) analyze institutional stability, and (5) predict transition outcomes.

## Core concepts

Transisi = transition; 'pemilihan' = election; 'pelantikan' = inauguration; 'kabinet' = cabinet; 'serah terima' = handover; 'kontinuitas' = continuity; 'perubahan' = change; 'instabilitas' = instability.

## Analytical methodology

Use KPU election data; analyze 'pelantikan' records; track 'kabinet' formation; examine 'serah terima' processes; assess policy continuity/change; monitor 'transisi daerah'.

## Step-by-step workflow

Step 1: identify transition period. Step 2: analyze process. Step 3: assess continuity/change. Step 4: identify risks. Step 5: predict outcomes.

## Data requirements

KPU election data; 'pelantikan' records; 'kabinet' documents; 'serah terima' reports; policy continuity studies; 'transisi daerah' data.

## Approved public sources

kpu.go.id; perpres.go.id; kemenkumham.go.id; kemendagri.go.id; each ministry's 'transisi' page. Verify transition data via official sources.

## Source verification standards

Cross-check election data with KPU; verify 'pelantikan' with official records; confirm 'kabinet' with Presidential Decrees.

## Analytical framework

Transition model: election → inauguration → cabinet → handover → continuity/change → stability. Identify risks.

## Evidence requirements

Election data, inauguration records, cabinet documents, handover reports, policy studies.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH for official KPU/Perpres data. MODERATE for transition assessments. LOW for speculative transition outcomes.

## Common analytical mistakes

Confusing transition with revolution; ignoring 'kontinuitas'; treating 'kabinet' as always new; missing 'transisi daerah'; relying on media transition coverage.

## Ethical considerations

Transition data is public. Avoid political bias; stick to official evidence.

## Security boundaries

Do not fabricate transition data or invent 'coup attempts' without official evidence.

## Expected output

Transition memos with process analysis, continuity/change assessment, risk identification, stability analysis, and outcome prediction. Cite KPU/Perpres data.

## Quality checklist

Period identified? Process analyzed? Continuity/change assessed? Risks identified? Outcomes predicted?
