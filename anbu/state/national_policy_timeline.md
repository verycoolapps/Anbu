# National Policy Timeline

State-domain intelligence file for the ANBU analytical repository. Focus: **national policy timeline** under Indonesia's constitutional order (UUD 1945) and its democratic institutions.

## Purpose

Create a timeline of Indonesia's national policies: major laws, regulations, and policy shifts from 1945 to present. This file supports analysts understanding policy evolution and historical context.

## Scope

Covers: 'UU' milestones (1945-present); 'perubahan UUD' (constitutional amendments); 'kabinet' changes; 'kebijakan' shifts (Orde Baru, Reformasi, Jokowi era); 'UU' major (Cipta Kerja, KPK Law, etc.); 'kebijakan fiskal' changes; 'kebijakan luar negeri' shifts; 'kebijakan otonomi daerah' evolution.

## Intelligence objectives

Enable analysts to (1) understand policy evolution, (2) identify turning points, (3) analyze policy continuity/change, (4) contextualize current policies, and (5) predict future trends.

## Core concepts

Kebijakan = policy; 'UU' = law; 'kabinet' = cabinet; 'kebijakan fiskal' = fiscal policy; 'otonomi daerah' = regional autonomy; 'Orde Baru' = New Order; 'Reformasi' = Reform era; 'Jokowi' era.

## Analytical methodology

Use peraturan.go.id for laws; track 'UU' amendments; analyze 'kabinet' changes; examine 'kebijakan' shifts; map 'UU' major milestones; assess policy continuity/change.

## Step-by-step workflow

Step 1: identify policy milestones. Step 2: trace evolution. Step 3: analyze shifts. Step 4: contextualize current. Step 5: predict trends.

## Data requirements

peraturan.go.id database; 'UU' texts; 'kabinet' records; 'kebijakan' documents; 'perubahan UUD' records; 'LKPP' historical reports.

## Approved public sources

peraturan.go.id; kemenkumham.go.id; dpr.go.id; bappenas.go.id; each ministry's 'kebijakan' page. Verify timeline via official sources.

## Source verification standards

Cross-check laws with peraturan.go.id; verify policy shifts with official documents; confirm cabinet changes with Presidential Decrees.

## Analytical framework

Timeline model: era → policy → shift → continuity → change → current → future. Identify patterns.

## Evidence requirements

Law texts, cabinet records, policy documents, constitutional amendments, performance reports.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH for official peraturan.go.id data. MODERATE for policy shift analyses. LOW for speculative future trends.

## Common analytical mistakes

Confusing policy with law; ignoring 'kabinet' changes; treating 'Orde Baru' as monolithic; missing 'Reformasi' complexity; relying on media policy summaries.

## Ethical considerations

Policy data is public. Avoid political bias; stick to official evidence.

## Security boundaries

Do not fabricate policy timelines or invent 'secret policies' without official documents.

## Expected output

Timeline memos with milestone identification, evolution tracing, shift analysis, current contextualization, and trend prediction. Cite peraturan.go.id data.

## Quality checklist

Milestones identified? Evolution traced? Shifts analyzed? Current contextualized? Trends predicted?
