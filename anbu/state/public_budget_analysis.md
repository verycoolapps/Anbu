# Public Budget Analysis

State-domain intelligence file for the ANBU analytical repository. Focus: **public budget analysis** under Indonesia's constitutional order (UUD 1945) and its democratic institutions.

## Purpose

Analyze Indonesia's public budget: APBN (national budget) and APBD (regional budgets), their structure, allocation, execution, and oversight. This file supports analysts examining government spending, fiscal policy, and budget transparency.

## Scope

Covers: APBN (Anggaran Pendapatan dan Belanja Negara); APBD (Anggaran Pendapatan dan Belanja Daerah); 'penerimaan negara' (state revenue); 'belanja negara' (state spending); 'defisit' (deficit); 'utang' (debt); 'subsidi' (subsidies); 'belanja pegawai' (personnel spending); 'belanja modal' (capital spending); 'transfer ke daerah' (transfers to regions); 'LKPP' (performance reports).

## Intelligence objectives

Enable analysts to (1) understand budget structure, (2) assess spending priorities, (3) evaluate execution, (4) identify fiscal risks, and (5) predict budget outcomes.

## Core concepts

APBN = national budget; APBD = regional budget; 'penerimaan negara' = state revenue; 'belanja negara' = state spending; 'defisit' = deficit; 'utang' = debt; 'subsidi' = subsidies; 'belanja pegawai' = personnel; 'belanja modal' = capital; 'transfer ke daerah' = transfers.

## Analytical methodology

Use Kemenkeu APBN/APBD documents; analyze 'Laporan Keuangan' (financial reports); track budget execution via 'realization' data; examine BPK audit findings; monitor 'LKPP' reports.

## Step-by-step workflow

Step 1: map budget structure. Step 2: assess spending priorities. Step 3: evaluate execution. Step 4: identify fiscal risks. Step 5: predict outcomes.

## Data requirements

APBN/APBD documents; 'Laporan Keuangan' reports; budget execution data; BPK audit findings; 'LKPP' reports; debt statistics.

## Approved public sources

kemenkeu.go.id; bpk.go.id; bappenas.go.id; bps.go.id. Verify budget data via official Kemenkeu/BPK sources, not news estimates.

## Source verification standards

Cross-check budget figures with Kemenkeu; verify execution with 'realization' data; confirm audit findings with BPK.

## Analytical framework

Budget analysis model: revenue → allocation → execution → oversight → outcome. Identify weak links.

## Evidence requirements

APBN/APBD documents, financial reports, execution data, audit findings, performance reports.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH for official Kemenkeu/BPK data. MODERATE for execution assessments. LOW for speculative fiscal predictions.

## Common analytical mistakes

Confusing APBN with APBD; ignoring 'transfer ke daerah'; treating budget as final (it can be revised); missing 'belanja pegawai' dominance; relying on media budget summaries.

## Ethical considerations

Budget data is public. Avoid political bias; stick to official evidence.

## Security boundaries

Do not fabricate budget figures or invent 'budget corruption' without audit evidence.

## Expected output

Budget analysis memos with structure assessment, spending priorities, execution evaluation, and risk identification. Cite Kemenkeu/BPK data.

## Quality checklist

Budget structure mapped? Spending assessed? Execution evaluated? Risks identified? Outcomes predicted?
