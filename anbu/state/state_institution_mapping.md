# State Institution Mapping

State-domain intelligence file for the ANBU analytical repository. Focus: **state institution mapping** under Indonesia's constitutional order (UUD 1945) and its democratic institutions.

## Purpose

Inventory and map Indonesia's state institutions across executive, legislative, judicial, and independent bodies. This file provides the master directory of which agency does what, its legal basis, leadership, and interdependencies. Essential for analysts tracking government reform, corruption cases, or policy implementation.

## Scope

Covers all three branches: Executive (President, Vice President, 30+ ministries K/L, Cabinet Secretariat, agencies like Bappenas, BKPM, NLC); Legislative (MPR, DPR, DPD); Judicial (MA, MK, KY, Jaksa Agung); Independent institutions (KPK, BKN, BPK, BPKP, KPU, Bawaslu, BI, OJK, BPS, LPS, KY). Does NOT cover private companies or NGO structures unless they function as state instruments.

## Intelligence objectives

Enable analysts to (1) identify which institution has jurisdiction over a given issue, (2) trace chains of command, (3) detect overlapping or conflicting mandates, (4) assess institutional capacity, and (5) predict how reorganization (Perpres) affects service delivery.

## Core concepts

State institution = legal entity with public authority; K/L = Kementerian/Lembaga (ministry/agency); independent institutions have constitutional or statutory basis; 'Lembaga' includes non-ministerial bodies; Cabinet (Kabinet) is the collective executive; Presidential Regulation (Perpres) can create/reorganize agencies.

## Analytical methodology

Use official organizational charts from each institution's website; cross-reference Perpres on ministry establishment; monitor 'reorganisasi' news; use BPS institution directory; track KPK investigations into agency corruption.

## Step-by-step workflow

Step 1: list institutions by branch. Step 2: assign legal basis (UUD, UU, Perpres). Step 3: identify leadership (minister, head, chief). Step 4: map reporting lines. Step 5: note recent reorganizations.

## Data requirements

Perpres establishing ministries; UU on independent institutions; official 'struktur organisasi' pages; BPS statistical agency directory; KPK case database; news on cabinet reshuffles.

## Approved public sources

Each ministry's official site (kemendag.go.id, kemenkeu.go.id, etc.); mpr.go.id, dpr.go.id, dpd.go.id; mahkamahagung.go.id, mkri.id; kpk.go.id, bkn.go.id, bpk.go.id, bpkp.go.id, kpu.go.id, bawaslu.go.id, bi.go.id, ojk.go.id, bps.go.id. Verify leadership changes via official announcements, not just news.

## Source verification standards

Confirm institution still exists after latest Perpres; verify minister names via Presidential Decree (Perpres); check independent institutions' constitutional basis before treating them as executive.

## Analytical framework

Branch-based taxonomy: Executive → Legislative → Judicial → Independent. Within each, classify by function (policy, service, oversight, audit, election).

## Evidence requirements

Official organizational charts, legal decrees, leadership appointment decrees, recent reorganization news with dates.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH for institutions with clear legal basis and current leadership. MODERATE for newly created or recently reorganized bodies. LOW for informal or disputed mandates.

## Common analytical mistakes

Assuming all 'Lembaga' are ministries; ignoring that some agencies report to President directly vs. through minister; missing independent institutions' constitutional independence; treating abolished agencies as active.

## Ethical considerations

Public institution data is not sensitive. However, avoid defamation when noting corruption investigations; stick to official KPK/BPK findings.

## Security boundaries

Do not fabricate ministry names or invent 'shadow agencies'. Verify Perpres numbers before citing reorganization.

## Expected output

Institution directory with legal basis, leadership, functions, and recent changes. Include confidence per institution.

## Quality checklist

All branches covered? Legal basis cited? Leadership current? Reorganizations noted? Independent institutions separated from executive?
