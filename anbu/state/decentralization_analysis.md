# Decentralization Analysis

State-domain intelligence file for the ANBU analytical repository. Focus: **decentralization analysis** under Indonesia's constitutional order (UUD 1945) and its democratic institutions.

## Purpose

Analyze Indonesia's decentralization policy: the transfer of authority from central to regional governments, its implementation, outcomes, and challenges. This file supports analysts examining 'otonomi daerah' effectiveness and 'desentralisasi' impact.

## Scope

Covers: Law 22/1999 and Law 23/2014 on regional government; 'otonomi daerah' implementation; 'urusan' (government affairs) distribution; 'perimbangan keuangan' (financial balance); 'dekonsentrasi' vs. 'desentralisasi'; 'recentralisasi' trends; 'pemekaran' (region splitting); 'otonomi khusus' (special autonomy) for Papua, Aceh, Yogyakarta.

## Intelligence objectives

Enable analysts to (1) understand decentralization framework, (2) assess implementation gaps, (3) evaluate outcomes, (4) identify challenges, and (5) predict decentralization trends.

## Core concepts

Desentralisasi = decentralization; 'otonomi daerah' = regional autonomy; 'urusan' = government affairs; 'perimbangan keuangan' = financial balance; 'pemekaran' = region splitting; 'otonomi khusus' = special autonomy; 'dekonsentrasi' = deconcentration; 'recentralisasi' = recentralization.

## Analytical methodology

Use Law 23/2014; analyze 'urusan' distribution; track 'perimbangan keuangan' allocations; examine 'pemekaran' cases; monitor 'otonomi khusus' implementation; assess BPS regional data.

## Step-by-step workflow

Step 1: map decentralization framework. Step 2: assess 'urusan' distribution. Step 3: evaluate 'perimbangan keuangan'. Step 4: identify challenges. Step 5: predict trends.

## Data requirements

Law 23/2014; 'urusan' distribution tables; 'perimbangan keuangan' data; 'pemekaran' statistics; 'otonomi khusus' laws; BPS regional indicators.

## Approved public sources

peraturan.go.id; kemendagri.go.id; bappenas.go.id; bps.go.id; kpu.go.id. Verify decentralization data via official sources.

## Source verification standards

Cross-check 'urusan' with Law 23/2014; verify 'perimbangan keuangan' with official allocations; confirm 'pemekaran' with Kemendagri.

## Analytical framework

Decentralization model: central → regional. Map 'urusan' distribution, 'perimbangan keuangan', and 'otonomi khusus' variations.

## Evidence requirements

Law 23/2014, 'urusan' tables, financial allocations, 'pemekaran' data, 'otonomi khusus' laws, BPS indicators.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH for legal framework and official data. MODERATE for outcome assessments. LOW for speculative decentralization predictions.

## Common analytical mistakes

Confusing desentralisasi with dekonsentrasi; assuming all regions have same autonomy; ignoring 'otonomi khusus'; treating 'pemekaran' as always beneficial; missing fiscal constraints.

## Ethical considerations

Decentralization data is public. Avoid regional bias; stick to official evidence.

## Security boundaries

Do not fabricate decentralization data or invent 'failed autonomy' without evidence.

## Expected output

Decentralization memos with framework analysis, implementation assessment, and trend prediction. Cite Law 23/2014/Kemendagri data.

## Quality checklist

Framework mapped? 'Urusan' assessed? Financial balance evaluated? Challenges identified? Trends predicted?
