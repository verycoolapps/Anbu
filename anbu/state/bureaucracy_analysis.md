# Bureaucracy Analysis

State-domain intelligence file for the ANBU analytical repository. Focus: **bureaucracy analysis** under Indonesia's constitutional order (UUD 1945) and its democratic institutions.

## Purpose

Analyze Indonesia's civil service (Aparatur Sipil Negara / ASN) system: recruitment, training, performance, corruption, and reform. This file supports analysts examining bureaucratic capacity, red tape, and administrative reform.

## Scope

Covers: ASN recruitment (CPNS/PPPK); training (Lembaga Pengembangan SDM); performance evaluation (kinerja); corruption in bureaucracy; 'birokrasi' reform under Jokowi; 'ASN' vs. 'PNS' distinction; 'eselon' structure; 'mutasi' (transfer) system; 'pangkat' (rank) system.

## Intelligence objectives

Enable analysts to (1) assess bureaucratic capacity, (2) identify corruption risks, (3) track reform progress, (4) evaluate service delivery, and (5) predict impact of civil service reforms.

## Core concepts

ASN = Aparatur Sipil Negara (civil servants); PNS = Pegawai Negeri Sipil (civil servants); PPPK = Pegawai Pemerintah dengan Perjanjian Kerja (contract workers); 'eselon' hierarchy; 'pangkat' rank system; 'mutasi' transfer; 'diklat' training; 'kinerja' performance.

## Analytical methodology

Use BKN (Badan Kepegawaian Negara) data; track KPK corruption cases involving ASN; analyze 'Laporan Kinerja' of ministries; monitor 'reformasi birokrasi' programs; examine 'Perpres' on ASN management.

## Step-by-step workflow

Step 1: map ASN system structure. Step 2: track recruitment and training. Step 3: assess performance and corruption. Step 4: evaluate reform programs. Step 5: identify bottlenecks.

## Data requirements

BKN statistical data; KPK case database; ministry performance reports; Perpres on ASN management; 'Laporan Reformasi Birokrasi' reports.

## Approved public sources

bkn.go.id; kpk.go.id; each ministry's 'kepegawaian' page; perpres.go.id; bpk.go.id. Verify ASN numbers via BKN, not news estimates.

## Source verification standards

Cross-check ASN statistics with BKN; verify KPK cases with official announcements; confirm reform programs with Perpres/UU.

## Analytical framework

Bureaucratic capacity model: recruitment → training → performance → accountability → reform. Identify weak links.

## Evidence requirements

BKN data, KPK cases, ministry performance reports, reform program documents.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH for official BKN/KPK data. MODERATE for performance assessments. LOW for anecdotal corruption claims.

## Common analytical mistakes

Assuming all ASN are PNS; ignoring PPPK growth; treating 'reformasi' as complete; missing regional variation; relying on non-official 'birokrasi' critiques.

## Ethical considerations

Civil service data is public. Avoid defamation when discussing corruption; stick to official KPK/BPK findings.

## Security boundaries

Do not fabricate ASN numbers or invent 'corruption networks'. Verify BKN data before citing.

## Expected output

Bureaucratic analysis memos with capacity assessment, corruption risk map, and reform progress. Cite BKN/KPK data.

## Quality checklist

ASN numbers verified? KPK cases cited? Reform programs tracked? Regional variation noted? Performance data current?
