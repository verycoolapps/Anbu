# Ministry Directory

State-domain intelligence file for the ANBU analytical repository. Focus: **ministry directory** under Indonesia's constitutional order (UUD 1945) and its democratic institutions.

## Purpose

Catalog all Indonesian ministries (Kementerian) and their subordinate agencies with functions, ministers, and key programs. This file is the master list for analysts tracking ministerial performance, budget allocation, or policy implementation.

## Scope

Covers all Kementerian under the current Cabinet: e.g., Kementerian Dalam Negeri (Kemendagri), Kementerian Luar Negeri (Kemlu), Kementerian Pertahanan (Kemhan), Kementerian Hukum dan HAM (Kemenkumham), Kementerian Keuangan (Kemenkeu), Kementerian PPN/Bappenas, Kementerian Perindustrian, Kementerian Perdagangan (Kemendag), Kementerian Pertanian, Kementerian Kehutanan, Kementerian Kelautan dan Perikanan, Kementerian Perhubungan, Kementerian Kesehatan, Kementerian Pendidikan, Kementerian Agama, Kementerian Sosial, Kementerian Ketenagakerjaan, Kementerian PU, Kementerian Perumahan, Kementerian Kominfo, Kementerian Pariwisata, Kementerian Koperasi, Kementerian UMKM, Kementerian Investasi/BKPM, Kementerian Pemberdayaan Perempuan, Kementerian Pemuda dan Olahraga, Kementerian Desa, Kementerian Reformasi Birokrasi, Kementerian ESDM, Kementerian Kelautan, and others as established by Perpres.

## Intelligence objectives

Enable analysts to (1) find which ministry handles a given issue, (2) track ministerial budgets and programs, (3) assess minister performance, (4) identify overlapping mandates, and (5) predict policy outcomes.

## Core concepts

Kementerian = ministry headed by a minister; 'K/L' = Kementerian/Lembaga; ministries are executive branch, headed by President; 'eselon' structure (Eselon I-III); 'Urusan Pemerintahan' (government affairs) under Law 23/2014.

## Analytical methodology

Use each ministry's official website for structure; cross-reference Perpres on establishment; track minister appointments via Presidential Decree; monitor ministry 'Rencana Strategis' (strategic plans) and 'Laporan Kinerja' (performance reports).

## Step-by-step workflow

Step 1: list all ministries. Step 2: assign functions and Urusan. Step 3: identify minister and key officials. Step 4: note subordinate agencies (Badan/Lembaga). Step 5: track recent performance reports.

## Data requirements

Perpres establishing ministries; UU on ministry functions; ministry strategic plans; LKPP (Laporan Kinerja Pemerintah Pusat) reports; BPK audit reports; news on minister reshuffles.

## Approved public sources

Each ministry's official site (kemendag.go.id, kemenkeu.go.id, kemenkes.go.id, etc.); perpres.go.id; bpk.go.id; bps.go.id. Verify minister names via official Presidential Decree, not just news.

## Source verification standards

Confirm ministry still exists under current Perpres; verify minister name via official decree; check that functions match Law 23/2014 Urusan assignment.

## Analytical framework

Functional taxonomy: economic, social, infrastructure, security, foreign affairs, internal affairs, coordination. Map each ministry to its Urusan.

## Evidence requirements

Perpres on establishment, ministry strategic plans, LKPP reports, BPK audit findings.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH for ministries with clear legal basis and current ministers. MODERATE for recently created or merged ministries. LOW for informal 'shadow ministries'.

## Common analytical mistakes

Missing 'Lembaga' that are not ministries; assuming all ministries have same structure; ignoring 'Kementerian' vs. 'Lembaga' distinction; treating abolished ministries as active.

## Ethical considerations

Ministry information is public. Avoid speculation about minister corruption without official findings; stick to KPK/BPK cases.

## Security boundaries

Do not fabricate ministry names or invent 'secret ministries'. Verify Perpres before citing reorganization.

## Expected output

Ministry directory with functions, ministers, subordinate agencies, and recent performance. Include confidence per ministry.

## Quality checklist

All ministries listed? Ministers current? Functions match Urusan? Subordinate agencies noted? Performance reports cited?
