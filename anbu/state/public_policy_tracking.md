# Public Policy Tracking

State-domain intelligence file for the ANBU analytical repository. Focus: **public policy tracking** under Indonesia's constitutional order (UUD 1945) and its democratic institutions.

## Purpose

Track Indonesia's public policies from formulation through implementation to evaluation. This file supports analysts monitoring policy effectiveness, government priorities, and reform outcomes across sectors.

## Scope

Covers all public policies at national and local levels: legislation (UU), government regulations (PP), presidential regulations (Perpres), ministerial regulations (Permen), local regulations (Perda); policy instruments (subsidies, taxes, programs); policy evaluation reports; 'RPJMN' (national development plan); 'RPJMD' (regional development plan).

## Intelligence objectives

Enable analysts to (1) track policy lifecycle, (2) assess implementation gaps, (3) evaluate outcomes, (4) identify policy failures, and (5) predict policy impacts.

## Core concepts

Public policy = 'kebijakan publik'; policy cycle = formulation → implementation → evaluation; 'RPJMN' = Rencana Pembangunan Jangka Menengah Nasional; 'RPJMD' = Rencana Pembangunan Jangka Menengah Daerah; 'program' = government program; 'kebijakan' = policy.

## Analytical methodology

Use peraturan.go.id for legal instruments; track 'Laporan Kinerja' for implementation; analyze 'evaluasi kebijakan' reports; monitor Bappenas development plans; examine 'LKPP' reports.

## Step-by-step workflow

Step 1: identify policy instrument. Step 2: trace formulation process. Step 3: assess implementation. Step 4: evaluate outcomes. Step 5: identify gaps.

## Data requirements

Peraturan (UU/PP/Perpres/Permen/Perda); RPJMN/RPJMD; ministry performance reports; policy evaluation reports; BPK audit findings.

## Approved public sources

peraturan.go.id; bappenas.go.id; each ministry's 'kebijakan' page; bpk.go.id; bps.go.id. Verify policy instruments via official legal database.

## Source verification standards

Cross-check policy instruments with peraturan.go.id; verify implementation with ministry reports; confirm evaluation with official findings.

## Analytical framework

Policy cycle model: agenda setting → formulation → adoption → implementation → evaluation → feedback.

## Evidence requirements

Legal instruments, development plans, performance reports, evaluation reports, audit findings.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH for legal instruments and official reports. MODERATE for implementation assessments. LOW for speculative policy impacts.

## Common analytical mistakes

Assuming policy = law (policy can be non-legal); ignoring implementation gaps; treating 'program' as policy; missing local variations; relying on media summaries.

## Ethical considerations

Policy data is public. Avoid political bias when evaluating policies; stick to evidence.

## Security boundaries

Do not fabricate policies or invent 'secret programs'. Verify legal instruments before citing.

## Expected output

Policy tracking memos with lifecycle analysis, implementation assessment, and outcome evaluation. Cite legal instruments.

## Quality checklist

Policy instrument identified? Formulation traced? Implementation assessed? Outcomes evaluated? Gaps documented?
