# Law And Regulation Comparison

State-domain intelligence file for the ANBU analytical repository. Focus: **law and regulation comparison** under Indonesia's constitutional order (UUD 1945) and its democratic institutions.

## Purpose

Compare and contrast Indonesia's laws and regulations: identify conflicts, overlaps, gaps, and harmonization needs. This file supports analysts examining 'peraturan yang bertentangan' and regulatory coherence.

## Scope

Covers: 'UU' vs. 'PP' vs. 'Perpres' vs. 'Permen' vs. 'Perda' hierarchy; 'peraturan yang bertentangan' (conflicting regulations); 'peraturan yang tumpang tindih' (overlapping); 'peraturan yang tidak sinkron' (inconsistent); 'harmonisasi' (harmonization) process; 'asas peraturan' (legal principles); 'peraturan pelaksana' (implementing regulations).

## Intelligence objectives

Enable analysts to (1) identify regulatory conflicts, (2) assess overlaps, (3) find gaps, (4) recommend harmonization, and (5) improve regulatory coherence.

## Core concepts

Peraturan = regulation; 'berlawanan' = conflicting; 'tumpang tindih' = overlapping; 'tidak sinkron' = inconsistent; 'harmonisasi' = harmonization; 'asas' = principle; 'pelaksana' = implementing; 'hierarki' = hierarchy.

## Analytical methodology

Use peraturan.go.id; analyze 'harmonisasi' reports; identify conflicts between UU/PP/Perpres; assess overlaps across ministries; find gaps in regulation; recommend harmonization.

## Step-by-step workflow

Step 1: identify regulations. Step 2: compare hierarchy. Step 3: find conflicts. Step 4: assess overlaps. Step 5: recommend harmonization.

## Data requirements

peraturan.go.id database; 'harmonisasi' reports; conflict analyses; overlap studies; gap assessments; 'LKPP' reports.

## Approved public sources

peraturan.go.id; kemenkumham.go.id; kemenkeu.go.id; each ministry's 'peraturan' page. Verify comparisons via official sources.

## Source verification standards

Cross-check regulations with peraturan.go.id; verify conflicts with official analyses; confirm harmonization with official reports.

## Analytical framework

Regulatory comparison model: hierarchy → conflict → overlap → gap → harmonization. Identify issues and solutions.

## Evidence requirements

Regulation texts, conflict analyses, overlap studies, gap assessments, harmonization reports.

## Confidence assessment

HIGH for official peraturan.go.id data. MODERATE for conflict assessments. LOW for speculative regulatory issues.

## Common analytical mistakes

Assuming all conflicts are errors; ignoring 'harmonisasi' progress; treating 'Perda' as national; missing 'asas' principles; relying on media regulatory critiques.

## Ethical considerations

Regulatory data is public. Avoid political bias; stick to official evidence.

## Security boundaries

Do not fabricate conflicts or invent 'regulatory chaos' without official evidence.

## Expected output

Regulatory comparison memos with conflict identification, overlap assessment, gap analysis, and harmonization recommendations. Cite peraturan.go.id data.

## Quality checklist

Regulations compared? Conflicts identified? Overlaps assessed? Gaps found? Harmonization recommended?
